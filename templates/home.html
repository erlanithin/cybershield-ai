Core Capabilities

Multi-Class AI Detection Suite

CyberShield AI deploys sophisticated NLP pipelines and supervised machine learning algorithms to isolate and classify nuanced online hostility.

1. Real-Time Text Detection

Instant analysis of single posts, direct messages, and comments with sub-second latency, providing confidence scores and granular risk ratings.

2. Hate Speech Identification

Identifies dehumanizing slurs, religious hatred, racism, xenophobia, and discriminatory hostility targeting protected identity attributes.

3. Toxic Comment Analysis

Pinpoints aggressive profanity, persistent insults, trolling, vulgar hostility, and attempts to suppress constructive civil discourse.

4. Threat Detection & Grievous Harm

Isolates direct and indirect threats of bodily violence, stalking, weapons, and self-harm incitement. Automatically triggers urgent safety guidance and escalation protocols.

5. AI-Powered Reporting & Evidence Archival

Enables victims and moderators to file structured incident reports with platform tags, evidence uploads, moderation tracking, and downloadable audit logs.
